About

Pâtisserie V. Marie

Welcome to Patisserie V. Marie, where the art of Cake Design meets the sinful pleasures of French Pastry.

We invite you to step through the doors of our Vaughan Parisian style boutique. Only the freshest, natural and quality ingredients are used for their assortment of elegant specialty cakes, French inspired pastries and confectionery delights. These one-of-a-kind creations are not only delicious to the palate but also luscious works of art.
