Our Fruit Cakes

A tradition of richness and elegance

Our fruit cakes are the crown jewel of Patisserie V. Marie. Each one is carefully crafted with a luxurious blend of premium dried fruits — cherries, berries, and candied citrus — folded into a rich, spiced cake that's been perfected through years of dedication.

Whether adorned with a cascade of fresh strawberries and cherries, or elegantly decorated with gold script for milestone celebrations, our fruit cakes are designed to be the centrepiece of your most memorable moments.

Our Story

Crafted with Passion

At Patisserie V. Marie, every dessert is a labour of love. From our signature fruit cakes — crowned with cascading fresh berries — to our delicate French macarons, each creation is handcrafted with the finest ingredients and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

Our Vaughan patisserie and cake school is a place where tradition meets artistry. Whether you're browsing our boutique for a sweet treat or planning the dessert table of your dreams, we bring the same level of care and creativity to every detail.
